## Initial direction
- **Problem:** Students can lose their plan when tasks take longer than expected or deadlines compete.
- **Target user:** College students who want a practical, low-friction way to plan and recover.
- **Product decision:** Make “Rescue My Day” the core interaction, rather than building a generic to-do list alone.
- **Initial scope:** Task CRUD, priority/deadline/estimate, dashboard, filters, persistent storage, recovery plan.
